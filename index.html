Connect your AI

Connect any provider with an OpenAI-compatible chat completions API. Your key stays on the server, not in the browser.

AI_API_KEY="your-key" \
AI_MODEL="your-model" \
AI_BASE_URL="https://provider.example/v1" \
python server.py

Replace the values with your provider’s actual details, then restart the server. Free tiers may have limits or change. AI output needs review.
